## About the Data

This project uses Warner Bros. Discovery's quarterly streaming revenue and subscriber data from 2024 to 2025. The dataset was collected from publicly available earnings reports and SEC filings.

The analysis covers eight quarters and focuses on revenue trends, subscriber growth, and comparisons between 2024 and 2025.

Since the dataset contains quarterly company-level figures rather than individual customer information, it cannot be used to measure customer churn.

The figures and their reporting definitions should be checked against the original reports before drawing final conclusions.
